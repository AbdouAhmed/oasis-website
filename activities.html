Safari & Adventures

Discover the hidden magic of the Western Desert with our expert local Bedouin guides.

White Desert Expedition

Jump into a 4x4 off-road vehicle for the thrill of a lifetime. Explore the surreal, snow-like chalk rock formations of the White Desert and the volcanic peaks of the Black Desert.

  • Guided 4x4 off-road tours
  • Sandboarding on massive dunes
  • Visits to natural cold and hot springs

Bedouin Nights & Stargazing

As the sun sets, the desert transforms into a magical starlit sanctuary. Gather around a crackling campfire on authentic kilim rugs, sip sweet Bedouin tea, and enjoy a traditional slow-cooked dinner.

  • Traditional underground-cooked meals
  • Live local Bedouin music
  • Unmatched stargazing away from city lights
